About me

Hi, I'm Your Name! I make bright, happy art, mostly flowers. Watercolor is my first love, but I'm always trying something new: linocut printing, handmade paper, acrylics, oil pastels and more.

I paint botanicals, with the occasional landscape or still life thrown in. My goal is simple: make colorful, cheerful pieces that make a home feel a little more fun.
